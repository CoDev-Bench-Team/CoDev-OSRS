compare. Every node's `editInfo.lastEditedAt` / `createdAt` after
**2026-09-24T05:59:31Z** (the baseline's export time) is rolled up to its
owning top-level frame. A frame with no node edited after that instant is
unchanged. The decoder ran from a scratch folder outside the repository
(`kiwi-schema`, `fzstd`), so the repository gained no dependency
(constitution VIII).

## 2. Not applied — `Received` spreads to the Employee's screens (BEN-134)

Every new node in the export is `Received`:

- `03.1`, `04.1` and `04.2` each gained a fifth `Status Timeline Item` reading
  **Received** · *Pending*, with a grey dot and a connector line
  (`Frame 55`, `Line 7`). The Employee's timeline is now
  Submitted → Approved → For Delivery/For Pickup → Complete → **Received**.
  Note the order: `Received` is drawn **after** `Complete`. The Status
  Definitions table of 09-24 §2, and the project owner's decision recorded in
  [spec 008](../../specs/008-request-review-panel/spec.md) (Clarifications,
  2026-09-26), put it **before** Complete.
- `Status changed email - Received` is a new email: the 600px card with a
  `Request Status` pill in `#ff8d28` (the `Received` variant), a request list
  of three rows, and a primary action.

The project owner decided on 2026-09-26 to adopt `Received`. That is
constitution **4.0.0**, and it lands through **BEN-134**, not here. Until then
`RequestStatus` stays the seven states of 3.0.1, and none of the above is built.

**Flagged to the designer**: the Employee frames put `Received` after
`Complete`. The owner's decision, and the Admin's `Complete` only being offered
once the Employee has signed, both need it before `Complete`.

## 4. Accepted as a correction — the timeline dots are coloured

The dots are not in the `Status Timeline Item` component. Each `Status Timeline`
frame draws them as separate 12×12 frames beside the items (`Frame 51`,
`Frame 50`, `Frame 53`, `Frame 54`). None was edited after the baseline. They
are drawn the same way in frames dating from the **09-22** export, such as
`02.2.1 - … - Update Status`. The SPA's `StatusTimeline` (spec 007) painted
every reached dot amber. That was a porting miss, not a design change.

| Node | Reached fill | Current-step halo | Seen in |
|------|--------------|-------------------|---------|
| Submitted | `#b4740e` (amber-600) | — (never current once moved on) | every timeline |
| Approved | `#2e7d46` (green-600) | 2px outside stroke, `#2e7d46` @ 10% | `02.2.1 - Update Status` |
| For Delivery | `#ef5da8` (pink-500) | 2px outside stroke, `#ef5da8` @ 10% | `02.2.1 - Update Status` (2), `Frame 75` |
| Rejected | `#c81e1e` (red-700); `#c62828` in `02.2.2.1` | 2px, `#c81e1e` @ 10% | `02.2.2.1`, `Frame 76` |
| Cancelled | `#4b5063` (ink-700) | 2px, `#4b5063` @ 10% | `04 - History`, `Frame 13` |
| Not reached | `#d8d7d2` — the build keeps `line-default` `#e3e6ec`, as the token layer has no `#d8d7d2` | — | every timeline |

Three of these are **not** the pill ink of the same status: Submitted is
amber-600 where the pill is amber-700, Approved green-600 where the pill is
green-700, and Rejected red-700 where the pill is red-600. The dots follow the
frames, not the pills.

**Inferred, not drawn**: no frame shows a reached **Ready for Pickup** or
**Complete** dot. They take their pill ink (blue-700, purple-600), and Complete's
halo uses `purple-50` because the token layer has no purple tint. Logged in
[additions.md](additions.md) §3g.

**Flagged to the designer**:

- `02.2.1 - … - Approve` draws the Approved dot **grey** with a green halo, while
  its label carries a date. Every other reached dot is filled. The build fills it.
- Rejected is `#c62828` in `02.2.2.1` and `#c81e1e` in `Frame 76`. That is the
  two-reds question again (09-22 §9). The build uses `#c81e1e`, the value of the
  `Status/Rejected` style (09-24 §9).

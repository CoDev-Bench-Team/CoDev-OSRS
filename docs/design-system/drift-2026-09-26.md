## How this was read

The method is the same as 09-22 and 09-24. A `.fig` is a zip whose
`canvas.fig` is Figma's kiwi binary: `fig-kiwi` magic, a version, then
length-prefixed blocks. Block 0 is the compiled schema (raw deflate) and block 1
is the document (zstd). The 09-24 file is no longer on disk, so this is not a
node-by-node compare. Every node's `editInfo.lastEditedAt` / `createdAt` after
**2026-09-24T05:59:31Z** (the baseline's export time) is rolled up to its owning
top-level frame. A frame with no node edited after that instant is unchanged.

Two reading rules apply to every port and every later diff:

1. **The style is the authority, not the cached value**
   ([drift-2026-09-22 §9](drift-2026-09-22.md#9-the-stale-cache-trap-reached-the-text-layer)).
   Resolve `styleIdForText`, `styleIdForFill` and `styleIdForStrokeFill` before
   reading `fontSize`, `fontName` or `fillPaints`.
2. **In a frame that draws a side panel over a page, only the panel is
   authoritative.** The page behind it is backdrop, and the designer does not
   keep it current. Set by the project owner, 2026-09-26. It is why the
   stock-line table behind `03.1 - Inventory - Add Inventory` is not evidence
   for Inventory frame B (§2), and why the Inventory header behind the eight
   `03.1 Add Asset - …` panels is not Assets copy (§4, #9).

## 3. Accepted — `Received`, a fifth step on every timeline (constitution 5.0.0; ADR-0009)

The three Employee frames edited on 09-26 — `03.1`, `04.1`, `04.2` — each
gained a `Status Timeline Item` reading **Received**, added between 01:01 and
01:46 UTC. Their timelines now have five steps:

`Submitted → Approved → For Delivery/For Pickup → Complete → Received`

In the timeline, `Received` comes **after** `Complete`. The Status Definitions
table (09-24 §2) defines it as happening before `Completed`. The file disagrees
with itself on the order. `04.1`'s Accountability Form view carries a dated
version of the same timeline (`Received · Sep 11, 2026, 9:42 AM`, `Complete`
pending).

The `Request Status` component's `Received` variant, which 09-24 §2 reported and
held back, is the pill for it: **`#ff8d28`** ink on a **10%** tint of the same
colour. Neither paint is bound to a style.

`Status changed email - Received` (new, 09-25) is the notification. It has the
eyebrow and headline **"Equipment Delivered/Claimed"**, the copy "Hi Maya - This
is a confirmation that IT has issued you these equipment. Before it's fully
checked out to you, please review and sign the accountability form confirming
you've received it.", a `Completed Sep 29, 2026, 9:42AM` row, an ITEM / QTY
list, and the action **Review & sign in the portal**.

### What the project owner decided

The file draws the step but not who takes it, nor where it falls. The owner
decided on 2026-09-26 (BEN-43):

| Question | Decision |
|----------|----------|
| Order? | `For Delivery` / `Ready for Pickup` → **`Received`** → `Completed`, as the Status Definitions table has it, not as the timeline draws it. |
| What moves a request to `Received`? | **The System**, when the owning Employee **submits the Accountability Form**. The form is submitted while the request is `For Delivery` or `Ready for Pickup`. |
| Who moves `Received` to `Completed`? | **The Admin**, with the Update Status panel's existing **Complete** action. |
| When do the items leave the store? | On **`Received`**. The request's reserved units move to `Assigned` (§2), so `Total` and `Reserved` fall by the requested quantity. `Completed` changes no unit. |

Two more follow from those, and were recorded as defaults rather than asked:

- **`Received` cannot be cancelled.** The items are with the Employee, as they
  are once `Completed`. Cancellation stays reachable from `Pending Approval`,
  `Approved`, `For Delivery` and `Ready for Pickup` only.
- **The form signs for request lines, not units.** `04.1` draws per-unit tags
  (`CODEV- LAPTOP-0212`). Which units a request holds is the API's decision
  (constitution III), and the contract does not expose them on a request, so
  the MVP form lists the request's items and quantities.

### What this reverses

- **ADR-0007 decision 2, in part.** The Admin still sets `Completed`. But the
  Employee now confirms receipt, which ADR-0007 withdrew and spec 001 FR-012a
  forbade. FR-012a is rewritten.
- **ADR-0008 / constitution 4.0.0 III's assignment point** moves from
  `Completed` to `Received`.
- **Queue (spec 004).** `Received` is live: it waits on the Admin's Complete, so
  the Requests Queue lists it. The queue's chips are unchanged in the file, so
  `Received` has **no chip**. It is counted under *All requests* and
  *In Processing*.

### Not applied

- **The Accountability Form itself** (`04.1`: the ten conditions, *I have read
  and agree to the above*, *Type full name to sign*, *Other Notes*,
  *I acknowledge and sign*). It is specced (spec 001 FR-012b) and tasked
  (tasks T018b), and needs the backend contract to expose the submission
  (contracts/README.md conflict 5).
- **Per-unit tags** on the form's lines, until the contract exposes a request's
  units.

## 4b. Accepted — timeline dots take their status colour (BEN-47)

Found while building the Admin review panel (spec 008). Each `Status Timeline`
draws its dots as separate 12×12 frames beside the items (`Frame 51`, `50`,
`53`, `54`), not inside `Status Timeline Item`. A reached dot is filled in its
status's colour, not always amber:

| Node | Drawn fill | Seen in |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Submitted | `#b4740e` | every timeline |
| Approved | `#2e7d46` | `02.2.1 - … - Update Status` |
| For Delivery | `#ef5da8` | `02.2.1 - … - Update Status` (2) |
| Rejected | `#c81e1e`, or `#c62828` in `02.2.2.1` | `02.2.2.1` |
| Cancelled | `#4b5063` | `04.2- … - Cancelled`, `04 - History` |

None of these dots was edited after 2026-09-24. They are drawn the same way in
frames dating from the 09-22 export. The SPA had painted every reached dot
amber, which was a porting miss, not a design change.

**Decided by the project owner, 2026-09-26: the dots follow the status pills.**
A reached dot takes the ink of the pill for the status it stands for:
Submitted `Pending Approval`, then Approved, For Delivery or Ready for Pickup,
Received, and Complete. The current step's halo is that ink at 10%. Three frame
shades differ slightly from the pill inks: Submitted `#b4740e` against the
pill's amber, Approved `#2e7d46` against `#16794a`, and Rejected `#c81e1e`
against `#c62828`. The pills win, so one status has one colour everywhere. No
frame draws a reached Ready for Pickup, Received or Complete dot, and those
follow their pills too.

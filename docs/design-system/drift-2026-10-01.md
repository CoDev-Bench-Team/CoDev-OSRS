## How this was read

is the compiled schema (raw deflate), block 1 the document (zstd). The 09-29
document is still on disk, so this is a **node-by-node compare**: every node
added, removed, re-texted, resized, re-parented or moved since 09-29, plus every
`editInfo.lastEditedAt` after **2026-09-29T04:09:17Z**.

the authority over the cached value, and in a frame that draws a side panel over

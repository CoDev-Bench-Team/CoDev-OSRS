## 1. `02.2` gains an optional **Other Notes** field

At the bottom of the review panel's body, under the STATUS timeline and above
*"The employee will receive an email with your decision."* and **Reject
Request** / **Approve Request**:

- a label, **Other Notes**, in the same small grey caption as the Request List
  drawer's *Note to Approver (optional)*;
- a bordered multi-line box, about 78px tall, sample text *"With laptop bag,
  mouse, mousepad and charger"*.

The label draws no asterisk. The project owner confirmed that the field is
optional, and the SPA labels it **Other Notes (optional)**, following the
drawer's convention for an optional note.

**Reading.** The field sits over the decision, so it is the Admin's note sent
with Approve or Reject. The sample text reads as a handover note (what goes
with the laptop). No frame reads the note back, and no email template has a
row for it.

## 2. What it is not

- It is not the Accountability Form's *Other Notes* (`04.1`, spec 001 FR-012b).
  That one belongs to the Employee and is sent when they sign for receipt. The
  two fields share a label and nothing else.
- It is not the rejection reason. The reason stays required and has its own
  block (`02.2.2`).

## 3. Contract

`PATCH /requests/{id}` (`UpdateRequestDto`: `purpose`, `status`,
`rejectionReason`) has no field for it. Recorded as
[contracts conflict 6](../../specs/001-office-supplies-mvp/contracts/README.md#6-the-admins-other-notes-on-a-decision-raised-2026-09-29).

## 4. For the designer

- Does the field appear on the other admin states (`02.2.1` Approve / Update
  Status, `02.2.2` Reject), or only on `Pending Approval`? The SPA shows it only
  where a decision is offered.
- Is the note read back anywhere: the panel after the decision, History, or the
  `Request approved` / `Request declined` email?
